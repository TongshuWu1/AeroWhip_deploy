(paper target RA-L, open-source release). The policy commands accelerations at 50 Hz through the
firmware flight pipeline. Task decisions approved 2026-10-28: `internal/agent_notes/aerowhip-task-decisions.md`.

## The fitting design (agreed) — to be rerun on the new drone

`internal/archive/fitting_and_adaptation_design.md`. Stages: 0 measurements (masses, attachment, marker
intervals from labels), 1 thrust map, 2 drone (drone-only flights), 3 cable + compact residual
(measured clamp, 1 s segments), 4 coupled validation (nothing fitted, per-flight bias cancelled,
stroke test is the yardstick), 5 randomisation, 6 freeze/train/fly. Each parameter is fitted only
from the data that sees it; coupled flights only validate. Decisions use held-out flights and
results that reproduced twice. No new bench measurements (user decision, cf_2): CoM, inertia, arm,
motor response nominal and randomised — revisit for the new frame, which can be measured as built.

## THE PLAN (2026-10-28 evening, agreed with the user; supersedes the open items below)

One round = one ~80 s flight + about an hour of computing; repeat until the held-out score stops
improving for two rounds, then freeze, rebuild the reset bank, train.
1. Plan: `scripts/optimize_trajectory.py --setup cf_6 --output runs/planning/excitation_NNN.json` (a
   free trajectory, normalised one level above what has flown cleanly, every ensemble member keeps
   the cable below the clamp, scored by the Fisher information's weakest parameter); fallback
   `scripts/plan_flight.py` (primitives). Flown with `--trajectory` (or `--plan`).
2. Fly: `python3 /workspace/scripts/acceleration_circle_cf2.py --trajectory /workspace/calibration_data/plans/<file>`
   (the container sees only the flight stack: the Identification Loop window copies the file into
   `calibration_data/plans/`; the flight folder keeps a copy as `trajectory.json`; lands itself on
   link loss or cable over the drone).
3. Measure: `scripts/envelope.py --setup cf_6`.
4. Fit: `label_sequence.py` on the flight; drone fit and cable fit `--windows strokes` (Model Fit
   window), warm-started, on all cf_6 flights.
5. Check: `validate_coupled.py` (stroke tip path, tip peak speed, add the cable rise), `closed_loop_check.py`.
All five steps are in one window: `python -m aerowhip.ui.planning` (Identification Loop: 3D preview
of each planned flight, its information and safety, the envelope; locks its computing while a flight
script runs). Round 1 is `runs/planning/excitation_001.json` (60 s at
3 m/s², weakest parameter the cable's static y P2, every member's cable ≥ 7 cm below the clamp;
optimised before the coupling report: re-optimise to see it). Trials and the primitive plan_001
are in `runs/planning/trials/`. The envelope is the
safety authority, not the simulator. Online estimator: built (2026-10-28 late; `scripts/online_fit.py --watch`, Online page); its estimate is the
running model the planner uses (runs/online/model_state.json; delay held; see the record). Parked until the loop has run twice:
streaming re-planning, differentiable engine, bench measurements. The planner's
random parts fail the motor-floor constraint for a physical reason (fast horizontal terms drive a
simulated motor to zero at low throttle; see the record, 2026-10-28 optimised excitation): not a bug.
Round 1 passes the flight script's own pre-arm area check (path x −0.26…0.29, y −0.18…0.20, height
1.35–2.06 m from a take-off at the origin) and its cable stays ≥ 0.32 m above the floor in the preview.
